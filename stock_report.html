The default scan prioritizes real backing behind a move: company headlines, major market/geopolitical news, catalyst strength, liquidity, options availability, and chart context. The first option target is +20%; losers should be cut around -25% to -30% or when the catalyst or chart trigger fails. Price data and headlines are fetched at run time and may be delayed or unavailable.
